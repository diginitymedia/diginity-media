1. General Policy

At Diginity Media, we are committed to delivering quality digital marketing services. Because our work involves strategy, time, and execution by our team from day one, our refund policy differs based on the type of engagement, as outlined below.

⚠️ Due to the nature of digital marketing work — strategy development, campaign setup, content creation, and ad platform fees — refunds are evaluated on a case-by-case basis and are not guaranteed for work already completed.

2. Monthly Retainer Services

ScenarioRefund Eligibility
Cancellation within first 7 days, before any work begins100% refund
Cancellation after work has started (strategy, setup, content)Pro-rated for unused days only; work delivered is non-refundable
Cancellation after 15+ days into the billing cycleNo refund for that cycle; service stops at cycle end
Dissatisfaction with results within agreed KPIs/timelineWe offer a revision/strategy reset before any refund discussion

Monthly retainers auto-renew unless cancelled with 15 days' written notice before the next billing date.

3. One-Time Projects

For one-time projects such as website development, branding, or a single campaign setup:

  • Before work starts: Full refund minus any payment gateway charges, within 3 business days of payment
  • After work starts (wireframes, designs, drafts shared): 50% of remaining project value may be refunded depending on work completed
  • After final delivery and approval: No refund — the deliverable has been accepted and handed over

4. Ad Spend & Media Budgets

Amounts paid directly to advertising platforms (Google Ads, Meta Ads, LinkedIn Ads, etc.) are non-refundable through Diginity Media, as these funds are paid to the platform, not to us. Our management fee for running these campaigns follows the retainer refund policy above.

5. Cancellation Process

  1. Send a written cancellation request to diginitymedia@gmail.com or via WhatsApp
  2. Our team will confirm receipt within 1 business day and outline next steps
  3. Any applicable refund will be processed within 7-10 business days to the original payment method
  4. Access to reports, dashboards, and ad accounts will be transitioned as per your service agreement

6. Non-Refundable Items

  • Third-party costs already paid (domain registration, premium plugins, stock assets, paid tools/subscriptions purchased on your behalf)
  • Ad spend already delivered to Google, Meta, or other ad platforms
  • Work that has been delivered, approved, and published live
  • Setup fees for tools, CRMs, or integrations already configured

7. Disputes & Escalation

If you are not satisfied with a refund decision, you may escalate the matter to our management team at diginitymedia@gmail.com with subject line "Refund Escalation." We aim to resolve all disputes amicably within 10 business days. This policy is governed by the laws of India, with jurisdiction in Bangalore, Karnataka.
