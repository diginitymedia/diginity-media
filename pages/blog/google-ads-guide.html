Google Ads for Indian Businesses 2026: Complete Setup & Strategy Guide

Google Ads is the most powerful lead generation tool available to Indian businesses — when set up correctly. The keyword is "correctly." Which Campaign Type Should You Start With?

  • Search campaigns: Best for lead generation (calls, form fills, bookings). Target people actively searching for your service. Start here.
  • Performance Max: Google's AI-driven campaign across all channels. Great for ecommerce and brands with conversion data. Not ideal for beginners.
  • Shopping campaigns: For ecommerce with a product feed. Shows your products with images and prices.
  • Display campaigns: Banner ads across websites. Good for retargeting. Too expensive for cold prospecting.
  • YouTube ads: Video ads. Best for awareness and remarketing, not direct lead generation.

Our recommendation for most Indian businesses: Start with Search campaigns. Once you have 50+ conversions/month, add Performance Max.

Step-by-Step Search Campaign Setup

Set Up Conversion Tracking First

This is non-negotiable. Running Google Ads without conversion tracking is like driving blindfolded. Set up before spending a single rupee.

  1. Go to Google Ads → Tools → Measurement → Conversions → + New Conversion
  2. Choose Website → Enter your conversion name (e.g. "Form Submission")
  3. Install the conversion tag via Google Tag Manager
  4. Test by submitting a test form — verify the conversion fires in Tag Manager Preview mode

Match Types — The Most Misunderstood Setting

  • Broad Match: Google shows your ad for any vaguely related query. Wastes budget. Use only with Target CPA or Target ROAS bidding and a large negative keyword list.
  • Phrase Match: Your ad shows when the search includes your keyword phrase. Good balance of reach and control. Use for most keywords.
  • [Exact Match]: Your ad shows only when someone searches that exact term. Lowest waste, least reach. Use for your highest-converting keywords.
⚠️ Biggest mistake: Running all keywords on broad match with no negative keywords. We've seen accounts where 80% of clicks came from completely irrelevant searches. Always use phrase or exact match when starting out.

Build a Negative Keyword List

Negative keywords prevent your ads from showing on irrelevant searches — saving budget and improving Quality Score.

Add these to every campaign immediately:

  • free, cheap, low cost, affordable (unless that's your offer)
  • jobs, careers, salary, internship (job seekers, not customers)
  • how to, tutorial, course, learn (information seekers, not buyers)
  • Competitor brand names (unless running competitor campaigns)

After your first week, review the Search Terms Report (Campaigns → Keywords → Search terms) and add more negatives weekly.

Write High-Converting Ad Copy

Google Responsive Search Ads (RSA) let you add up to 15 headlines and 4 descriptions. Google tests combinations automatically.

Headlines structure (fill all 15):

  • 3 with your exact keyword: "SEO Agency Bangalore" / "Digital Marketing Bangalore"
  • 3 with your USP: "150+ Brands Scaled" / "8× Average ROI" / "98% Client Retention"
  • 3 with CTAs: "Get Free Audit Today" / "Book Free Strategy Call" / "See Our Results"
  • 3 with features: "No Lock-in Contracts" / "Weekly Reporting" / "Dedicated Manager"
  • 3 with social proof: "4.9★ Google Rating" / "₹50Cr+ Revenue Generated"

Description tips: Lead with the strongest benefit. Include a clear CTA. Mention your location for local relevance. Use ad customisers for urgency ("Limited slots this month").

Bidding Strategy — What to Use When

  • Manual CPC: Use for the first 2–4 weeks to collect data. Full control, no automation.
  • Maximize Clicks: Good for building volume when starting out and cost is less of a concern.
  • Target CPA: Use once you have 30+ conversions/month. Tell Google your target cost per lead/sale.
  • Target ROAS: For ecommerce with purchase values tracked. Requires 50+ conversions/month.
  • Maximize Conversions: Good middle ground — Google optimises for most conversions within your budget.
Recommended path: Maximize Clicks (weeks 1–3) → Maximize Conversions (weeks 4–8) → Target CPA (month 3+, once you have enough data)

Budget Recommendations for India

  • Testing budget: ₹15,000–25,000/month. Enough to test 2–3 ad groups and gather meaningful data.
  • Minimum viable: ₹30,000/month for most service businesses to see consistent leads
  • Scale phase: ₹75,000–2,00,000/month once you've proven a positive ROAS
  • CPC expectations (India): SEO/marketing services ₹40–120, Real estate ₹80–250, Healthcare ₹30–100, Education ₹20–60, Ecommerce ₹5–30

Weekly Optimisation Checklist

  1. Review Search Terms Report — add new negatives
  2. Check Quality Scores — below 6/10 means landing page or ad relevance needs fixing
  3. Pause keywords with 100+ clicks, zero conversions
  4. Increase bids on keywords with good ROAS
  5. Review Ad Strength — aim for "Excellent" on all RSAs
  6. Check impression share — below 50% means budget or bid is limiting reach
