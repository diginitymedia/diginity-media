Local SEO Checklist 2026: Rank #1 on Google Maps in 90 Days

Customers searching "best gym near me" or "dentist in Koramangala" are ready to buy. This checklist tells you exactly how to get there.

Why Local SEO Is Different from Regular SEO

Regular SEO targets keywords like "best running shoes India." Local SEO targets "running shoes store near me" or "running shoes store Koramangala." The searcher has local intent — they want to visit or call a nearby business.

Google shows three types of local results: Local Pack (Maps) — the 3 businesses shown in a map at the top of results, Local Organic — regular blue link results with local relevance, and Google Maps — results inside maps.google.com. This guide focuses on winning the Local Pack — the most valuable position.

The 4 Ranking Factors Google Uses for Local Pack

  1. Relevance — how well your Google Business Profile matches the search query
  2. Distance — how close your business is to the searcher (you can't change this)
  3. Prominence — how well-known and authoritative your business is online
  4. User signals — clicks, calls, direction requests, and review engagement

This checklist addresses everything except distance. Every item strengthens at least one of these signals.

Part 1 — Google Business Profile (GBP)

GBP is the single most important factor for local ranking. An incomplete or unoptimised profile will never rank — no matter how good everything else is.

📍 Google Business Profile Checklist

✓
Claim and verify your GBP listing — go to business.google.com, claim your listing, verify via postcard, phone, or email
✓
Business name matches exactly — same name on your website, signage, and all directories. No keyword stuffing in the name.
✓
Select the most specific primary category — e.g. "Fitness Centre" not just "Gym." Add 2-3 secondary categories.
✓
Write a keyword-rich description — 750 characters, mention your services, location (neighbourhood + city), and unique selling point
✓
Add all services and products — every service you offer with descriptions and prices where relevant
✓
Add accurate business hours — including holiday hours. Closed = wrong hours = lost customers and ranking penalty
✓
Upload 20+ high-quality photos — exterior, interior, team, products/services, logo. Google rewards active photo uploads.
✓
Add your website URL — and a booking/appointment link if relevant
✓
Answer all Q&A questions — proactively add your own Q&As with keywords. Answer any questions customers ask.
✓
Post weekly GBP updates — offers, news, events, new products. Consistent posting = higher ranking signals

Part 2 — Reviews Strategy

Reviews are the #1 prominence signal. Businesses with 50+ recent reviews at 4.5★+ almost always outrank competitors with fewer reviews — even if other signals are weaker.

⭐ Reviews Checklist

✓
Create a short review link — Google Business → Get more reviews → copy the direct link
✓
Train your front desk/team — ask every satisfied customer for a review. The ask should happen in person while they're happy.
✓
Send post-visit WhatsApp — 2 hours after a service, send: "Hope you enjoyed! It would mean a lot if you shared a quick Google review: [link]"
✓
Add QR code at checkout — print a small card or sign with a QR code linking to your review page
✓
Respond to every review — good and bad. Google rewards businesses that respond. Use keywords naturally in your responses.
✓
Never incentivise reviews — against Google's policy. Ask genuinely, don't offer discounts for reviews.
✓
Target: 10 new reviews/month — consistency matters more than getting 50 in one week. Steady growth = better ranking signal.

Part 3 — Citation Building

Citations are any online mention of your business Name, Address, and Phone number (NAP). Google uses citations to verify your business is real and where you say it is. Inconsistent NAP = ranking penalty.

🗂 Citation Checklist — Indian Directories

✓
Justdial — justdial.com (highest priority for Indian local SEO)
✓
Sulekha — sulekha.com
✓
IndiaMART — indiamart.com (for B2B businesses)
✓
Practo — practo.com (for healthcare businesses)
✓
TradeIndia — tradeindia.com
✓
Bing Places — bingplaces.com (yes, still matters)
✓
Apple Maps Connect — mapsconnect.apple.com (iPhone users!)
✓
Facebook Business Page — with correct address and phone
✓
LinkedIn Company Page — with business address
✓
Exactlywhat NAP consistency check — verify name, address, phone is identical across all platforms. Even "St" vs "Street" matters.

Part 4 — On-Page Local SEO

Your website sends strong local ranking signals when properly optimised. These changes take 30 minutes to implement.

🌐 Website Local SEO Checklist

✓
Add LocalBusiness schema — already done in your Diginity website. Verify at search.google.com/test/rich-results
✓
NAP in website footer — add your business name, full address, and phone number in the website footer (text, not image)
✓
Create a dedicated Contact page — with embedded Google Map, full address, and phone number
✓
Location-specific page titles — "Digital Marketing Agency in Koramangala, Bangalore" not just "Services"
✓
Write neighbourhood content — mention nearby landmarks, areas, and pin codes naturally in your content
✓
Create location pages (multi-location) — if you have 2+ locations, create a unique page for each with specific content

90-Day Local SEO Timeline

  1. Week 1: Claim GBP, complete all fields, add 20 photos, write description, add services
  2. Week 2: Start citation building (10 directories), launch review request process
  3. Week 3–4: First 10 reviews live, website schema verified, location pages updated
  4. Month 2: Continue reviews (20 total), add GBP weekly posts, build more citations
  5. Month 3: 30+ reviews, 30+ citations, weekly GBP posts — start seeing Map Pack appearances
Benchmark: Most local businesses with a clear geographic target see Map Pack appearances within 60 days and Top 3 ranking within 90 days, assuming they complete this full checklist consistently.
