WhatsApp Business App vs WhatsApp Business API

📱 WhatsApp Business App

  • Free to use
  • Manual messages only
  • Max 256 contacts per broadcast
  • No automation possible
  • Good for: Small businesses, 0–500 contacts

⚡ WhatsApp Business API

  • Paid (via BSPs like Interakt, Wati, AiSensy)
  • Full automation — triggers, flows, chatbots
  • Unlimited contacts
  • CRM integration possible
  • Good for: Growing brands, 500+ contacts
Recommendation: Start with the free WhatsApp Business App if you have under 200 active customers. Switch to API (via Interakt or AiSensy — both India-based, starts ₹2,500/month) when you hit 200+ contacts or want cart abandonment automation.

3. Broadcast Campaigns (Promotions)

The most common use — but most brands do it wrong. Key rules:

  • Segment your list: Never blast everyone the same message. New customers, repeat buyers, and VIPs should get different messages
  • Timing matters: Best open rates in India: 8–10am, 12–2pm, and 7–9pm. Avoid Monday morning and Friday evening.
  • Keep it conversational: Write like a friend, not a brand. Short paragraphs. Emojis used sparingly.
  • One clear CTA: One link, one action. Multiple CTAs reduce conversions.
  • Frequency: Max 2–3 broadcasts per week. More than that and opt-outs spike.

5. Event / Launch Campaigns

Build anticipation before a product launch, sale, or event using a 3-message sequence:

  1. 5 days before: Teaser — "Something exciting is coming... 👀"
  2. 1 day before: Reveal + early access link for existing customers
  3. Day of: Live announcement with direct purchase/booking link

WhatsApp Marketing Best Practices for 2026

  • Always get opt-in consent — collect WhatsApp numbers explicitly with a consent checkbox. No opt-in = risk of being reported and banned.
  • Use quick replies — set up pre-configured reply buttons in the API for common responses (Yes/No, interested/not interested)
  • Integrate with CRM — tag contacts by purchase history, interest, or stage so your messages are always relevant
  • Monitor opt-out rate — if more than 2% opt out from a broadcast, your message was wrong. A/B test content and timing.
  • Never buy lists — purchased contact lists violate WhatsApp's policies and will get your number banned
  • Use WhatsApp Catalog — for product businesses, set up a product catalog in your WhatsApp Business profile so customers can browse and order without leaving WhatsApp
