The 3-Stage Meta Ads Funnel

Top of Funnel (TOF)

Awareness & Prospecting

Cold audiences who don't know you exist. Goal: introduce your brand and get them to engage or visit your site. Metrics: CPM, reach, video view rate, landing page views.

Middle of Funnel (MOF)

Consideration & Engagement

People who've engaged with your content, watched your video, or visited your site. Goal: build trust and interest. Metrics: add-to-cart rate, initiate checkout, lead form fills.

Bottom of Funnel (BOF)

Conversion & Retargeting

Warm audiences who've visited your site, added to cart, or initiated checkout. Goal: get the purchase. Metrics: ROAS, CPP, conversion rate.

Stage 1 — Top of Funnel: Prospecting

Audiences to Test

  • Interest-based: 3-5 specific interests related to your product (not broad categories)
  • Lookalike audiences: 1% lookalike of your purchasers or high-value website visitors
  • Broad audience: Age + gender only — Meta's algorithm often outperforms interest targeting for established pixels

Best TOF Creative Formats

  • Reels (15–30 seconds): Hook in first 2 seconds, problem-solution format, CTA at 80% of video
  • Carousel: Feature 5–7 products or benefits, first card does the heavy lifting
  • UGC-style videos: Authentic customer testimonial format outperforms polished brand ads 3:1
💡 The 2-second hook rule: 60% of users who see your video stop scrolling within 2 seconds or skip. Your first 2 seconds must show the product, demonstrate the problem, or make a bold claim. Do not start with your logo.

Stage 2 — Middle of Funnel: Engagement Retargeting

Audiences

  • 95% video viewers from TOF campaigns
  • Instagram profile engagers (last 30 days)
  • Facebook page engagers (last 30 days)
  • Website visitors who didn't add to cart (last 30 days)

Best MOF Creative

  • Social proof: reviews, testimonials, before/after content
  • Product benefits deep-dive: address top 3 objections
  • Behind-the-scenes / founder story content builds trust

Stage 3 — Bottom of Funnel: Conversion Retargeting

Audiences (highest priority)

  • Cart abandoners (last 7 days) — highest intent, hottest audience
  • Checkout initiators (last 14 days) — left at last step
  • Product page viewers, no add-to-cart (last 14 days)
  • Past purchasers (30–180 days) — for repeat purchase or upsell

BOF Creative Strategy

  • Direct offer: "Still thinking about it? Free shipping today only"
  • Social proof + urgency: "847 people bought this week · Only 12 left"
  • Dynamic Product Ads (DPA): show the exact product they viewed

The Most Common Meta Ads Mistakes

  1. One campaign for everything: TOF and BOF in the same campaign — Meta's algorithm gets confused, performance suffers
  2. Too many ad sets: More than 5–6 ad sets in one campaign causes audience overlap and budget fragmentation
  3. Turning off campaigns too fast: Meta needs 50 conversion events per ad set to exit the learning phase — don't judge in the first 7 days
  4. Weak creatives: No amount of targeting optimisation fixes bad creative. Creative is 70% of performance.
  5. Not tracking properly: No Pixel + CAPI = blind optimisation. iOS 14+ made server-side tracking essential.
  6. Ignoring frequency: When frequency hits 4+ for TOF audiences, refresh creatives immediately. Ad fatigue kills ROAS fast.
